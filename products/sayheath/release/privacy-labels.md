# SayHeath 1.0.0 (1) — App Privacy questionnaire worksheet

Use the signed iOS candidate and US service to verify the answers before publishing the questionnaire. The English privacy policy is independent of the public store description.

|Collected by SayHeath|Apple category|Purpose|Linked to account|
|---|---|---|---|
|Sign-in email, Family Care recipient email and support reply email|Contact Info → Email Address|App Functionality|Yes (include support conservatively)|
|US account identifier and bilateral sharing/consent records|Identifiers → User ID; Other Data where applicable|App Functionality|Yes|
|Steps and other retained physical activity summaries|Health & Fitness → Fitness|App Functionality|Yes|
|Sleep, newly authorized blood pressure, oxygen and heart rate|Health & Fitness → Health|App Functionality|Yes|
|Account-scoped wearable identifier used for sync and capture receipts|Identifiers → Device ID|App Functionality|Yes|
|Support form message|User Content → Customer Support|App Functionality|Yes (may contain a reply email)|

Pulse history and schedule values are local to this iPhone/wearable and are not sent as health records. Permission for those features is stored with the account. No advertising or tracking purpose is implemented. Before finalizing, check embedded SDK manifests, network behavior, actual US access-log retention and support/diagnostics. Do not declare that the app collects no data. Do not publish labels or declare the export answer from this worksheet alone.
