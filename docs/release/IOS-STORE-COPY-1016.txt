SUBTITLE
Connect your devices

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Connect a compatible device to review activity and sleep, keep your profile up to date, and choose which activity or sleep records to share with family.

DESCRIPTION
SayDian is a companion app for activity and sleep tracking with compatible devices, including supported smart rings and watches.

Review your daily steps and sleep duration. Distance, estimated energy expenditure, sleep stages and workout details are available only when your connected device actually provides them. Missing or unsupported data is not presented as a measurement.

Connect your device over Bluetooth, check its connection and battery status, and sync available activity and sleep records. Device controls and workout options vary by device. Keep your device nearby and the app open when syncing. Repeated uploads use record identifiers to avoid duplicate records.

Manage your nickname, avatar and personal profile. With Remote Care, invite family members and choose which activity or sleep categories to share. Sharing requires the participating accounts and their permission; it is not emergency monitoring.

Read general educational content in Health library when published articles are available in your language. This public reading feature does not evaluate your personal health records or provide individual medical advice.

The iOS edition supports activity and sleep only. It does not provide physiological measurements, disease-risk assessments, medical alerts, AI health advice or paid health-analysis reports. A compatible device is required for device-generated activity and sleep data. You can sign in, manage your account and access help without a device.

Bluetooth access is used to connect the device. Photo or camera access is optional for features such as a profile picture; location may be used for supported phone-weather and workout-route features when you grant permission. Manage optional permissions in iOS Settings. Account deletion is available in the app under Profile > Account settings.

Activity and sleep estimates are for general wellness reference, not diagnosis, treatment, emergency services or medical decision support. Consult a doctor before making any medical decisions. If you feel unwell, seek medical care.
