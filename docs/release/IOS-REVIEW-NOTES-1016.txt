Product scope
SayDian for iOS is now an activity-and-sleep companion. This scope is fixed for all iOS users and build modes; it is not an account-specific, location-specific, remote-config or review-only restriction.
The iOS UI, controller, device-command integration and upload boundaries restrict the edition to steps, distance, estimated calories and sleep duration/stages where the connected device supplies them. Physiological measurements, calibration controls, ECG, clinical/risk interpretation, medical alerts, general AI chat and paid AI health reports are unavailable. Sports views do not show heart-rate data. Remote Care permits activity/sleep categories only.

Health library is a public educational reader, separate from personal health processing. It does not analyze or transmit a user's health records or enable physiological measurements or medical alerts. Articles depend on published language availability; missing translations are not replaced with fabricated content.
Old local records are retained rather than erased. Historical physiological records, sleep scores/risk fields, physiological companion fields and waveforms are not displayed or uploaded by this build. AI requests are blocked before network transmission. We have not supplied medical accuracy or regulatory evidence and do not claim this edition as a medical device.

Sign-in and account review
Open SayDian. Use the email/password sign-in flow with the credentials supplied in Apple's App Review Information fields. This flow does not require an SMS or email code. A device is not required for sign-in, personal profile, account settings, support, privacy/terms or account deletion. Without a compatible device, device-generated data is unavailable rather than fabricated.

Device connection and activity/sleep review
Use a compatible device (including supported LuckRing and QRing models) that supports this app's Bluetooth integration. Keep it powered on and nearby, and ensure it is not connected to another companion app. After signing in, open the Device tab, choose Search for devices, grant Bluetooth access and select the actual device from the scan list. Connection and sync status are shown in the app. Only features and records actually reported by the connected model are shown.

Keep the app in the foreground for a manual sync. Upload success requires server acknowledgement. Unconfirmed allowed records remain locally pending. Repeated sync deduplicates confirmed records; restricted historical records remain preserved locally.

Health safety
The public description and localized in-app notice state that this edition is for general wellness reference, not diagnosis, treatment, emergency services or medical decision support. Consult a doctor before medical decisions and seek care if unwell.

Privacy and account deletion
Profile > Account settings contains the in-app account deletion flow. Privacy, terms and support are available in the app and at the international URLs below. Data is sent only to the international /global service; no domestic fallback is used. Data-sharing permissions are controlled by the participating accounts. The new iOS build does not send health data to an AI service.

Build 1016 processing notes
Bundled BluetoothLibrary includes unused Apple Health API references. This app does not request Apple Health access or enable HealthKit. The required Info.plist purpose strings explicitly describe this inactive integration. Activity and sleep sync uses the connected device directly.

Privacy
First-party service data is stored on servers in the United States. The English privacy policy and terms, version global-appstore-2026-10-14-r2, are reviewed and published. Current-location weather sends rounded coordinates to the international weather service as disclosed.
