# SAYDIAN Health Privacy Notice

**Effective date: October 1, 2026**

This Privacy Notice explains how SAYDIAN Health processes personal data. The
service is operated by the developer identified for SAYDIAN Health in the App
Store ("SAYDIAN", "we", "us", or "our").

## 1. Data we process

We process account information you provide, such as an email address or phone
number, nickname, language, and account-security information.

We process wellness and activity information that you choose to synchronize
from a compatible wearable, such as activity, sleep, and device-reported health
or fitness readings. These readings are for personal wellness tracking only.

We process wearable and app information needed to provide connected features,
including a wearable identifier, model, firmware, supported capabilities,
connection status, and—only when reported as a verified hardware address—the
wearable MAC address.

If you submit feedback, we process the category, message, optional contact
details, and any file you choose to attach. If you set a profile image or use an
upload feature, we process the selected image or file for that feature.

If push notifications are configured for your version of the app and you opt
in, we process a generated installation identifier, the push-provider
registration identifier, platform, app version, and locale to deliver those
notifications.

## 2. Optional permissions and on-device features

Bluetooth is used to find, connect to, and exchange information with a
compatible wearable.

Location is optional. When you choose current-location weather, the app uses
your location to request weather for that location from the configured weather
service. We do not send that location to the SAYDIAN Health service for this
feature.

Camera and photo-library access are optional. They are used when you choose a
profile image, upload evidence, create a photo watch face, or use camera-remote
functions. A selected profile image, upload, or evidence file is sent to our
service only when you confirm that feature; a watch-face image can instead be
sent directly to the connected wearable.

Contacts access is optional. It is used only for contacts you choose to manage
or transfer to a compatible wearable. The contact feature is not used for
marketing or advertising.

## 3. Why we use data

We use data to create and secure accounts, operate wearable connection and
sync, display your selected information, provide support, respond to feedback,
deliver enabled notifications, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use wellness data for advertising or
cross-app tracking.

## 4. Service providers and disclosures

We use service providers only where necessary to operate a feature. Depending
on the feature you choose, this can include a weather provider for weather
requests and a push-notification provider for enabled notifications.

We may disclose information where required by law or when reasonably necessary
to protect users, prevent fraud, or protect the service. We do not authorize
service providers to use your personal data for their own advertising.

## 5. Retention, security, and transfers

We keep account, wearable, and synchronized information while your account is
active, and afterwards only as needed for security, dispute handling, or legal
obligations. Retention can vary by data type and applicable law.

We use access controls and encrypted connections designed to protect data in
transit. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Our service providers or infrastructure may process data in locations different
from your own. Where applicable, we use safeguards required for those transfers.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can decline optional permissions, change them in iOS Settings, and disable
notifications in app or system settings. You can request access, correction, or
deletion of your account information through the app's account settings or the
support route below.

Deleting an account may remove access to associated data. Limited information
may remain for the period needed to meet security or legal obligations.

## 7. Children

SAYDIAN Health is not directed to children. If you are not old enough to
consent under the law that applies to you, use the service only with a parent or
legal guardian's authorization.

## 8. Changes and contact

We may update this Notice when the service or applicable law changes. The
effective date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions or requests, use **Help and feedback** in the app or the
support page listed on the SAYDIAN Health App Store product page.
